# Agency Dinner Outreach Process — Demand Gen x Events & Partnerships

## Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end process for how Demand Gen supports Events & Partnerships in inviting prospects to our agency dinners. The goal is to give the partnerships manager a clear, lightweight intake process and free them from having to build lists and sequences manually — so they can focus on relationships while we handle the execution.

We're planning to support 3 agency dinners this quarter. Based on the results, we'll decide together how to proceed in future quarters.

---

## Process at a Glance

| Step | Owner | Target Turnaround | Output |
|------|-------|--------------------|--------|
| 1. Submit event intake form | Partnerships Manager | 8 weeks before event | Completed intake form with event details |
| 2. Build prospect list | Demand Gen | After intake form is received | Prospect list for review |
| 3. Review & approve prospect list | Partnerships Manager | 48 hours after receiving list | Approved list (with any exclusions flagged) |
| 4. Create outreach sequence | Demand Gen | In parallel with or after step 2 | Draft sequence for review |
| 5. Review & approve outreach sequence | Partnerships Manager | 48 hours after receiving sequence | Approved sequence |
| 6. Set up sequence in HubSpot | Demand Gen | After approval | Sequence ready for enrollment |
| 7. Enroll contacts in sequence | Partnerships Manager | 48–72 hours after sequence is created | Sequence live |
| 8. Report on results | Partnerships Manager | Within 1 week after the agency dinner | Results report |

---

## Detailed Process

### Step 1: Event Intake Form

The partnerships manager kicks off the process by completing the intake form below. This should be done **8 weeks before the event** — and ideally, we'd have a heads-up that an event might happen at least 3 months in advance so we can plan accordingly.

The form is designed to be quick: wherever possible, just drop links to existing internal documents rather than writing things out from scratch. We'll pull what we need from there.

> See **Appendix: Event Intake Form Template** below.

### Step 2: Demand Gen Builds the Prospect List

Based on the intake form, Demand Gen builds a prospect list aligned to the target audience and event details. If the partnerships manager selected a manual outreach approach (calls, LinkedIn), Demand Gen also handles any contact data enrichment needed (mobile numbers, LinkedIn profiles, etc.).

### Step 3: Partnerships Manager Reviews the Prospect List

The partnerships manager reviews the list for:

- **Quality** — are these the right agencies / contacts for this event?
- **Exclusions** — flag any agencies you'd like to remove because you plan to reach out to them personally (that outreach is then your responsibility)

**Target turnaround: 48 hours.**

### Step 4: Demand Gen Creates the Outreach Sequence

Based on the approved list and event details, Demand Gen drafts the outreach sequence. The sequence type (fully automated emails vs. manual touchpoints like calls and LinkedIn) is determined by the partnerships manager's selection in the intake form.

The outreach will be sent from the partnerships manager's email/inbox unless specified otherwise.

### Step 5: Partnerships Manager Reviews the Outreach Sequence

Review the sequence to make sure messaging, tone, and details are accurate.

**Target turnaround: 48 hours.**

### Step 6: Demand Gen Sets Up the Sequence in HubSpot

Once approved, Demand Gen creates the sequence in HubSpot and prepares it for enrollment.

### Step 7: Partnerships Manager Enrolls Contacts

As a final check before going live, the partnerships manager enrolls the approved contacts into the sequence in HubSpot. This is the last quality gate before outreach begins.

**Target turnaround: 48–72 hours after the sequence is created.**

### Step 8: Post-Event Results Report

Within **1 week after the agency dinner**, the partnerships manager reports back on the results — specifically for prospects who came through this outreach campaign (not the event as a whole). This is essential for us to close feedback loops and improve future campaigns.

Metrics to include:

- Replies received
- Positive replies
- Confirmed attendees
- Meetings booked with the sales team
- Agency partner program sign-ups
- Deals and MRR attributed to this outreach

---

## Timeline Expectations

To make this work well for everyone, we're asking that reviews and approvals aim to happen within the proposed turnaround times above. Given that many of these events come together quickly, keeping momentum through each step helps us deliver the best possible outreach and avoid last-minute rushes.

If at any point something is blocked or a turnaround can't be met, just flag it — we'll figure it out together.

**1. Accompanying Main Event**

> _Response / links here_

**2. About the Agency Dinner**
What is this agency dinner about? Why would a prospect want to attend over doing anything else? Please provide as much detail as possible and link to any internal documents.

> _Response / links here_

**3. Target Audience**
Who is the target agency audience for this specific dinner? Why are they the right people to invite? (e.g., agency size, specialty, region, relevance to the event theme)

> _Response / links here_

**4. Capacity & Attendees**
How many prospects do we want to invite / is there space for? Who else will be attending (Kinsta team members, partners, speakers, etc.)?

> _Response / links here_

**5. Event Logistics**
Location, format, agenda, and any other logistical details about the agency dinner.

> _Response / links here_

**6. Anything Else for the Outreach**
Is there anything specific to this event that you think we should reference in the outreach — something we wouldn't otherwise know about? Anything you want prospects to know?

> _Response / links here_

**7. Confirmed Attendees**
Who has already confirmed they're attending?

> _Response / links here_

**8. Outreach Approach**
Should the outreach be:
- [ ] Fully automated (email-only sequence)
- [ ] Manual / blended (includes calls, LinkedIn connection requests, etc.)

_Note: If manual/blended is selected, Demand Gen will handle the additional data enrichment required (mobile numbers, LinkedIn profiles)._

**9. Timeline**
When is the agency dinner? _(Reminder: this form should be submitted 8 weeks before the event. Ideally, flag upcoming events 3 months in advance.)_
