# Variant Copy

Ready-to-use draft copy for the on-site tools and A/B tests. All of it promotes the **existing** "first month free" trial and free-migration offer — **nothing new to align on internally**.

> Voice: confident, concrete, low-hype. Kinsta's edge is performance + genuinely free managed migrations + fast expert support. Lead with those, not adjectives.
> Compliance check before shipping: confirm "first month free," "no credit card," and "free migration" are all currently true for the plans the CTA points to.

---

## 1. Sticky bar (OptinMonster, site-wide)

Pick one to start; A/B the rest.

- **A (offer-led):** Get your **first month free** on managed WordPress hosting. → **Start free**
- **B (migration-led):** Switch to Kinsta — **free migration + first month free**. → **Start free**
- **C (performance-led):** Faster WordPress, managed for you. **First month free.** → **Try Kinsta**
- **D (risk-reversal):** **No credit card. First month free.** Cancel anytime. → **Start now**

*Keep it one line; reserve its height in layout to avoid CLS.*

## 5. Copy notes

- **One benefit per element.** Don't stack "fast + free + migration + support" into a single button.
- **"Free" does heavy lifting** — the two strongest levers are *first month free* and *free migration*. Test which leads.
- **Match the click to the promise:** if the bar says "free migration," the destination should reinforce migration, not drop the visitor on a generic pricing grid.
- Generate more variants at volume with **Sonnet 5 @ medium**; keep final selection/judgment on **Opus 4.8 @ high**.
